Patient Management:
The system allows patients to register, update their profiles, search for doctors, check availability, and book appointments.

Appointment Management:
Patients can select a doctor, choose an available time slot, confirm appointments, and receive notifications.

Payment Management:
The platform supports online appointment payments and maintains transaction records.

Notifications:
Email and real-time notifications are sent when appointments are booked, updated, cancelled, or completed.

Security:
The application uses JWT authentication, role-based authorization, input validation, and secure API communication.

Database:
PostgreSQL stores hospital, doctor, patient, appointment, and payment information.
